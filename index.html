HOW IT WORKS

Three Simple Steps

01

Donate Food

Donors add details of their surplus food.

02

Request Food

NGOs browse and request available donations.

03

Collect Food

Approved donations are collected by the NGO.

OUR IMPACT

Every Meal Counts

🍲

Fresh surplus, shared fast

Food is listed with its expiry time so it reaches people while it is still good.

🤝

Verified approvals

Donors approve or reject each request, so food goes to the right hands.

🔒

No double booking

Once food is booked, others see it as "Already Booked".
